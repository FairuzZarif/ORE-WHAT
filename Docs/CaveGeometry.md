# Cave geometry and geology pass

Implemented in `PlayerTest` on 2026-10-04. The full Island Map rebuild reproduces this pass. No Git commit was made. Crystal ore models and the resource economy remain unchanged.

1. **Why the caves looked smooth.** The shell already had separate vertices and flat face normals. Its underlying elliptical rooms, capsule tunnels, smoothly interpolated noise and blended joins produced rounded silhouettes. Adding smaller rocks would not address that shape.

2. **Base geometry.** `CaveSpace.Distance` now blends broad planar room envelopes and angular tunnel cross-sections into the existing carving. Excavated Old Mine/Deep Mine sections have stronger cut shoulders; natural Crystal/Rift sections use a different envelope. Piecewise linear tetrahedral noise and tilted strata introduce large changes of slope. Features span roughly 5.5 m in tunnels, 7 m in rooms and 9 m in the boss cavern/Rift. Room locations, dimensions, tunnel endpoints, depth progression and topology are preserved. The original shell limits outward carving, keeping partition walls intact. Authored floors, plateau ramps and descending steps retain their original calculation; a clearance envelope protects the walking core, stepped routes and intentional crouch passage.

3. **Normals and shading.** Flat normals were retained on the shell and used on every new formation. No smoothing pass, noisy texture, extra shader or normal map was added. The existing matte rock materials, warm lamps, player headlamp and depth atmosphere light the larger planes. Ten shared light/dark material variants provide restrained layer contrast across five existing rock palettes.

4. **Modular assets.** Six reusable meshes and six prefabs are generated in `Assets/Models/Island/Geology` and `Assets/Prefabs/CaveGeology`: `WallSlab` (60 triangles), `JaggedWall` (80), `CeilingCluster` (80), `SmallRubble` (80), `MediumRubble` (100), `CollapseCluster` (100). Each cluster is one renderer/mesh with three shared material slots. All six mesh variants are instantiated in this rebuild. Existing Loafbrr rock forms, timber, rails, carts, equipment, wall fins and boss-perimeter rocks remain part of the environment.

5. **Wall, ceiling and floor variation.** Geometry changes alter the upper wall and ceiling silhouettes themselves. Selected wall slabs and jagged forms partially embed in the shell; ceiling formations vary height and scale. The floor keeps its broad existing roll, ramps and steps, with edge rubble rather than extra bumps in the walking lane. Both cave air and actual generated mesh raycasts are checked: every individual rock in a new cluster must intersect the visible shell, preventing detached wall/ceiling lobes. Placement has a fixed name-based seed and per-space budget. It does not run during gameplay.

6. **Rubble.** Small and medium clusters are floor raycast, aligned to the surface and seated into it near the wall. Dense or obstructed candidates are skipped. All existing prop bounds, route reserves, other new formations and ore reserves are checked before placement. Small/medium rubble and ceiling decoration have no collider. Wall formations and the large collapse cluster use their exact visible mesh; no oversized box or invisible ceiling is introduced. This rebuild places 73 wall formations, 129 ceiling formations and 91 rubble clusters, with 74 additional shared-mesh colliders.

7. **Timber and rails.** Timber spacing varies deterministically instead of using a perfectly uniform interval. Heights follow the rebuilt shell. Ceiling probes across the entire cap width and depth keep the beam ends out of sloping rock. Mouth frames receive equivalent clearance probes; wide openings use individually roof-bracing posts. Existing broken/sagging sets remain intentional mining wreckage. The rail generation already followed actual floor samples at two-metre intervals; it regenerates against the new floor and keeps its existing grounded sleepers, broken stretches, carts and branches. Rails add no collision surface.

8. **Old Mine.** The adit, office, stope, abandoned workings, lower drifts, shaft gallery, work area and approach now share the angular shell treatment and selective geology dressing. Their existing historical equipment, signage, loops, storeroom, ore pocket and old-incline shortcut remain. Old Mine receives the full placement budget; the other mines receive roughly half. The three other identities retain their distinct industrial, crystal and Rift palettes and infrastructure.

9. **Boss arena.** Its original large footprint, elevation, entrance, perimeter cover and winding-headframe landmark remain. The shell has broader angular planes and larger feature spacing. Larger wall/ceiling formations break up the perimeter and roof. New low formations are rejected within the existing 18 m combat radius plus a two-metre placement buffer. The floor and both entrance directions were walked successfully; no boss, encounter or gameplay gate was added.

10. **Ore protection.** The full rebuild generates ore sockets before the new geology pass. Every potential socket reserves 1.2 m around the node's outward surface centre and 1 m around its standing approach, using conservative renderer bounds. Reserved route and boss areas also remain protected. The final map has 663 valid sockets: Old Mine 295, Deep Mine 112, Crystal Caverns 81, Rift 175. Active targets remain 30/24/20/20 (94 total), with the same weights, rare caps, depth rules, prices and timers. Crystal models were not edited.

11. **Approximate triangle budget.** These are static mesh triangle instances under Island, counted in Edit Mode. Terrain, players, items and live ore instances are excluded; this is not a frame-time benchmark.

    | Geometry | Before | After |
    | --- | ---: | ---: |
    | Generated shell | 199,411 | 197,275 |
    | New geology instances | 0 | 23,320 |
    | All Island static mesh instances | 312,960 | 328,348 |

    The total increase is 15,388 triangles, approximately **4.9%**. The shell still uses 32 chunks at the original two-metre voxel size. The new formations contain 500 triangles across their six source meshes, reused across instances.

12. **Meshes/materials and rebuild behavior.** Six new mesh assets, six prefabs and ten new shared materials; six source meshes and fifteen shared material references are used by the 293 placed formations. Prefabs carry static batching/occlusion flags. No texture, per-rock update component or runtime placement loop was added. New serialized geology controls are initialized once per CaveSpace; subsequent rebuilds preserve inspector tuning. `Review Cave Geometry` reports triangle instances, shared resources, ore reservations and collider agreement. Existing asset GUIDs are preserved when meshes regenerate.

14. **Remaining art considerations.** The lower walking band, intentional crawl and stepped routes retain more of the old profile to preserve clearance through the coarse two-metre mesh. The combat disk remains comparatively plain and open. Close views can reveal seams where modular formations meet the coarse shell; targeted manual tuning could soften those seams without adding smaller rock spam. Large formation placement deliberately skips crowded areas around rails, stations and ores. The builder reports 32 fitted timber/steel sets, below its older density target of 40: some narrow roof shoulders reject caps after the new full-width clearance check. Those stretches could benefit from individually tuned narrower frames. Missing dressing candidates are not forced into player clearance.
